Physarum polycephalum

multi-agent model, Baumgarten · Jones · Hauser 2015
Click / drag to drop oat flakes. Shift + click (or Slime mode) to inoculate.
Mass comes only from oats: particles divide where they feed and at the growing front, and starve away over time. The colony follows the scent, relocates onto each oat it reaches, and leaves thinning veins behind.
